## About

I am an **Applied Scientist 2 at [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/)**, where I adapt and distill language models for product applications. My current work focuses on efficient post-training, synthetic training data, and large-scale evaluation for expanding model capabilities across new tasks and domains.

Previously I was a **Research Fellow at [Microsoft Research India](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-india/)**, advised by [Dr. Sunayana Sitaram](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/susitara/), [Dr. Satya Lokam](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/satya/) and [Dr. Navin Goyal](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/navingo/). There I worked on multilingual parameter-efficient finetuning, continual learning and catastrophic forgetting, language adaptation and cross-lingual transfer, and multilingual benchmarking — extending language-model capabilities to new languages and domains through efficient, modular methods.

I have also collaborated with [Dr. Vivek Gupta](https://vgupta123.github.io/), [Dr. Anoop Kunchukuttan (AI4Bharat)](https://ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in/) and [Dr. Ashwini Vaidya (IIT Delhi)](https://web.iitd.ac.in/~avaidya/) on benchmark datasets, multilingual inference, semantic parsing, and adapting language models for Indian languages. Earlier I held applied NLP roles at **American Express AI Labs** and **Builder.ai**, and I graduated from [Delhi Technological University](https://dtu.ac.in/) in 2021.

> I'm always happy to talk about research or collaborations — reach me at **[divyanshuggrwl@gmail.com](mailto:divyanshuggrwl@gmail.com)**.
